Certificate Request Process

As a Certificate Requester, you need to:

  1. Generate a private key and CSR on your system
  2. Submit the CSR through this form
  3. Wait for a Certificate Manager to review and approve
  4. Download your signed certificate once approved

How to Generate a CSR

Use OpenSSL to generate a private key and CSR:

# Generate private key
openssl genrsa -out private.key 2048

# Generate CSR
openssl req -new -key private.key -out request.csr \
  -subj "/CN=example.com/O=My Organization"

Keep your private key secure! Never share it with anyone.
